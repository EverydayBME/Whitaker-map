Paul Donaldson

When optics meets cell biology

Paul Donaldson’s research investigated the physiology of the eye’s lens, including questions related to cataract and presbyopia. International collaboration brought complementary perspectives into that work.

The work

In the interview, he recalled meeting a future Whitaker fellow at a conference in Kona. The resulting visit connected expertise in optics and mechanics with the laboratory’s lens-biology research.

The perspective

The collaboration illustrated how a conversation can grow into a sustained scientific relationship. Researchers with different skills could approach the same organ from different directions, helping one another identify questions and methods that would be harder to develop alone.
