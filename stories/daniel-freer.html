Daniel Freer

Engineering assistance around human signals

Daniel Freer’s Whitaker experience supported a master’s program in medical robotics and image-guided intervention at Imperial. It became part of a longer path into doctoral research.

Institution / community
Imperial College London
Role at the time
PhD researcher, Hamlyn Centre; former Whitaker fellow

The work

His projects included wearable assistance for tremor and work with EEG-based brain–computer interfaces. Shared-control approaches raised questions about how a person and a robotic system could contribute together to a task.

The perspective

Freer’s story connects an international training opportunity with the freedom to explore different applications. The projects shared an interest in human signals and robotic assistance, while opening possibilities beyond any one device or clinical setting.
