Anna Blakney & Robin Shattock

Building vaccines across disciplinary boundaries

Anna Blakney and Robin Shattock discussed vaccine research at Imperial in April 2019. Their conversation brought engineering approaches to delivery together with biological questions about immune responses.

Archive date
29 April 2019
Institution / community
Imperial College London
Role at the time
Postdoctoral researcher and vaccine research leader

The work

Blakney’s path included Whitaker-supported postdoctoral research and work on lipid nanoparticles for RNA vaccine delivery, including HIV-related research. Shattock described the challenges of translating vaccine and antiviral ideas into approaches that could be manufactured and made accessible.

The perspective

The joint interview emphasized complementary expertise and room for creative thinking. This conversation predates the COVID-19 pandemic; it records the research questions and ambitions of that time, without attributing later developments to the interview.
