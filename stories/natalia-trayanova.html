Natalia Trayanova

Building a scientific path without a ready-made route

Natalia Trayanova’s interview explored the personal circumstances behind an international scientific career. Her move from Bulgaria to the United States took place under constraints very different from a planned student exchange.

Read the edited interview →

Archive date
17 July 2019
Institution / community
Johns Hopkins University
Role at the time
Researcher and academic mentor

The work

She described a strong early interest in physics and the challenge of finding her own way in a new academic environment. The account resisted a simple story in which each career step followed from a freely chosen, carefully arranged plan.

The perspective

Looking at academic life from the perspective of a research leader, she also described the tension between organizational responsibilities and time for science. Her story adds another dimension to the archive: international careers can be shaped by historical circumstances, persistence, and opportunities that were difficult to anticipate.
