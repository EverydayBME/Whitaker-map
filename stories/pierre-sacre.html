Interview · EverydayBME

Pierre Sacré

Fundamental questions, future possibilities

Pierre Sacré worked at the interface of neural systems and artificial devices. His academic path included a Fulbright experience from Belgium to the United States in 2014 and postdoctoral research at Johns Hopkins.

Read the edited interview →

Archive date
24 November 2019
Institution / community
University of Liège
Role at the time
Assistant professor, electrical engineering and computer science

The work

His research combined nonlinear dynamical systems, statistical methods, and data science to connect observed neural activity and behavior with the mechanisms that produce them. It was work grounded in fundamental questions, rather than an immediately available treatment.

The perspective

Sacré described potential future benefits for patients as a motivation for doing that foundational work. His perspective connected mathematical and engineering methods with a long view of impact: discoveries may become useful through the work of other researchers as well as one’s own.
