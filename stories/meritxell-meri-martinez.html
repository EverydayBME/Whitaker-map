Meritxell “Meri” Martinez

Making biomedical engineering easier to discover

Meritxell “Meri” Martinez graduated in biomedical engineering from the University of Texas at San Antonio in May 2020. In her interview, she described exploring product development alongside a commitment to explaining the field to others.

Read the edited interview →

Archive date
28 August 2020
Institution / community
University of Texas at San Antonio
Role at the time
Biomedical engineering graduate; product-development engineering intern

The work

Her internship at Progenerative Medical involved validation testing related to orthopedic devices. It offered a view of how an engineering idea is examined in a product-development setting, alongside questions about business and regulation.

The perspective

She also co-founded BME Life in 2018 to share information, advice, and community around biomedical engineering. Her story joins two kinds of translation: moving technical work toward a product and making an unfamiliar profession understandable to people considering it.
