The experience

Ryan Geiser’s Whitaker experience at Cambridge became part of a doctoral path at the Centre for Misfolding Diseases. He remembered Chris Dobson not only for scientific guidance but also for generosity, encouragement, and personal support.

The perspective

His reflection described a research community extending beyond the laboratory, through college life, rowing, travel, and friendships across cultures. Those relationships helped make an unfamiliar place feel like home and became part of his growth as a scientist.
