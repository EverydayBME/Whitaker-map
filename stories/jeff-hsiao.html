Jeff Hsiao

Working where microfluidics meets molecular design

Jeff Hsiao described research in Andrew deMello’s group at ETH Zurich following a Whitaker experience. His work brought together microfluidics and questions about engineered nanoparticles.

The work

The interview discussed DNA-coated nanoparticles and their interactions with enzymes, with an interest in possible diagnostic applications. These projects connected the design of small-scale systems with biological behavior that needed to be measured and understood.

The perspective

His experience illustrates an international research path built through laboratory work and new technical questions. Moving between engineering methods and molecular biology offered opportunities to develop skills that did not sit neatly within a single discipline.
