Colin Hisey

Building a research life across borders

Colin Hisey trained in chemical engineering before building a research career around extracellular vesicles. A Whitaker experience in Spain’s Basque Country helped make international research a lasting part of his life.

Archive date

Institution / community
University of Auckland

The work

At Auckland, he supported researchers through training, experiment planning, and scientific meetings while developing his own projects. One focus was a microfluidic platform to capture selected extracellular vesicles from small biological samples for further analysis.

The perspective

His move to New Zealand followed a job application made while finishing his doctorate. In reflecting on international science, he emphasized relationships built through working together day after day. Those connections—and time spent exploring a new home beyond the laboratory—were central to his experience.
