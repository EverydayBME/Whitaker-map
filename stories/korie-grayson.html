Korie Grayson

From manufacturing questions to research

Korie Grayson’s interest in research grew through experience manufacturing medical devices. Seeing a dialysis graft in that setting prompted questions about how products are developed—and how she could contribute to that process.

The work

Her path included chemistry and premedical studies, followed by a biomedical engineering doctorate at Cornell. Courses and research in drug delivery helped connect her interests in biology, materials, and design. At the time of the interview, she was a postdoctoral researcher in chemical engineering at Michigan.

The perspective

Her story shows how work outside an academic laboratory can help clarify a research direction. Questions raised by an existing medical product became a reason to seek the training needed to investigate and develop new ideas.
