The experience

Joseph Yu’s Whitaker experience at Imperial helped develop more than technical expertise. In his alumni reflection, he connected international education with empathy and a more nuanced approach to communication.

The perspective

Identifying a worthwhile research question was a central challenge for him. The experience of working across cultural expectations also made clear that effective collaboration depends on listening and learning how other people communicate.
