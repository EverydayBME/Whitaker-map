Yadi Chen

From engineering training to the biology of vision

Yadi Chen’s studies took her from China to New Zealand and from biomedical engineering into physiology. At Auckland, she investigated the eye’s lens and questions connected to age-related eye disease.

The work

A major part of the doctoral journey involved building an experimental system before applying it to biological questions. That process joined practical engineering skills with cellular physiology and optics.

The perspective

Her story illustrates the less visible work behind a research result: developing the tools, learning how a system behaves, and only then using it to investigate disease. Moving between disciplines gave her different ways to approach the same problem.

“By binding engineering with the medical aspects, it is a very unique way to learn about the basics of engineering and also to get into a part of the medical field.”

— Yadi Chen, archived interview excerpt
