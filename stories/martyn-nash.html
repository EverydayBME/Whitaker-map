Martyn Nash

Reading the mechanics of living tissue

Martyn “Marty” Nash’s research connected medical images with the mechanics of soft tissues. At Auckland, personalized biomechanical models offered a way to ask questions that images alone could not answer.

The work

His work combined three-dimensional MRI or ultrasound information with mechanical loads to investigate properties such as heart-muscle elasticity and contractility. Related questions involved the way breast tissue deforms during imaging.

The perspective

Nash’s interview described movement between different kinds of science, including mechanics and electrophysiology. Keeping options open and learning new methods allowed his work to develop across disciplinary boundaries, linking mathematical models with biological measurements.
