Vijay Rajagopal

Zooming in from organs to cells

Vijay Rajagopal’s research connected the structure of a cell with its mechanical behavior. His route into that work began with multiscale and multiphysics modeling at the Auckland Bioengineering Institute.

The work

Experience modeling whole organs led to questions at a smaller scale. His Cell Structure and Mechanobiology Group investigated how the organization of cells relates to their function, bringing engineering approaches to biological complexity.

The perspective

He described inspiration from learning about the machinery inside living cells. Moving between scales—from an organ to the structures within a cell—offered new ways to formulate a problem and new connections between computation and biology.
