Jess Snedeker

Creating connections between engineering and orthopedics

Jess Snedeker’s role connected engineering research at ETH Zurich with orthopedic medicine at the University of Zurich. Working across those settings placed collaboration at the center of his scientific life.

The work

He described coordinating basic science within an orthopedic research environment. That position involved relating engineering approaches to questions arising in medicine and connecting people whose expertise came from different training traditions.

The perspective

The interview offers a view of interdisciplinary research as an ongoing practice. Building useful connections requires more than bringing disciplines into the same institution: it depends on people learning how to ask questions and work together across their boundaries.
