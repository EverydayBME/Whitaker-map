Giorgia Marchesi

Choosing a path by experiencing other places

For Giorgia Marchesi, international study was a way to explore both research and the kind of life she wanted to build. Her neuroengineering education included experiences in the Netherlands and the United States.

The work

During her master’s training, she spent time at Radboud University through Erasmus and pursued thesis research at the City College of New York School of Medicine. These experiences exposed her to different educational cultures and approaches to scientific work.

The perspective

Marchesi was drawn to projects with a meaningful connection to people’s lives. Working in different places helped her test her interests through experience, rather than choosing a future entirely in the abstract. Her story highlights curiosity as a practical tool for making career decisions.

“When you have the chance to do it, you do it.”

— Giorgia Marchesi, archived interview excerpt
