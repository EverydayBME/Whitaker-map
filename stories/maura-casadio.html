Maura Casadio

Finding a shared language for rehabilitation

Maura Casadio’s route into rehabilitation research connected electronic engineering, robotics, and an international postdoctoral experience. At Genoa, her work brought engineers into conversation with the people who understand rehabilitation from the clinical side.

The work

After studying in Pisa and completing a doctorate in Genoa, she worked at the Rehabilitation Institute of Chicago and Northwestern University. Her research interests included neural control, rehabilitation robotics, and body–machine interfaces: technologies that require both engineering insight and an understanding of human movement.

The perspective

Casadio emphasized the importance of a common language among engineers, therapists, and psychologists. She also valued research environments where students could ask difficult questions. Supportive mentorship and open discussion helped shape the collaborative approach she brought back to her own teaching and research.
