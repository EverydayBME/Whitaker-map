Ton van der Steen

Following sound into cardiovascular research

Ton van der Steen’s interest in sound and applied physics developed into a career in biomedical engineering. His path connected training in Delft and Nijmegen with cardiovascular research in Rotterdam.

Archive date
9 May 2019
Institution / community
Erasmus MC (Erasmus University Medical Center)
Role at the time

The work

At Erasmus MC, his group investigated ultrasound and optical approaches relevant to diagnosis and therapy. Engineering methods were developed in conversation with medical questions, requiring collaboration across technical and clinical expertise.

The perspective

His interview linked scientific direction with personal interests and the influence of research environments. A fascination with how sound works became part of a larger effort to create useful ways of examining and interacting with the cardiovascular system.
