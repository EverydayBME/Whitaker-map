Federico Barban

Engineering a connection to the nervous system

Federico Barban chose engineering as his way into questions about the body and the brain. His biomedical engineering studies at Genoa developed into doctoral work in neuroprosthetics.

The work

In the interview, he described research involving electrical brain stimulation in a rat model of stroke. The work linked engineering methods with experimental neuroscience, exploring questions relevant to recovery rather than presenting a finished clinical treatment.

The perspective

Hands-on teaching helped spark his interest in science, while experience abroad broadened his perspective beyond the laboratory. He valued work that felt meaningful and a career in which engineering could contribute to understanding—and eventually helping—people.
