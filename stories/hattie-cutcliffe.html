The experience

Hattie Cutcliffe’s Whitaker experience took her to the SAFER research environment at Chalmers, working with Karin Brolin. Her reflection also recognized the influence of her home mentor, Lou DeFrate.

The perspective

Writing as a Duke postdoctoral researcher in 2020, she discussed learning to work with uncertainty and to advocate for herself as a scientist. The experience abroad formed part of that growth, alongside the technical questions that shaped her research.
