Azade Allahyari

A path from medical devices to tissue engineering

Azade Allahyari’s international path connected work with biomedical devices in Iran, graduate study in Germany, and a research experience in Melbourne. Each stage offered another way to work at the interface of engineering and medicine.

The work

In Melbourne, she described research involving biofabrication, tissue engineering, and decellularization. The experience moved her closer to experimental questions about biological materials after earlier work with medical products.

The perspective

Her interview presents a career shaped through transitions between countries and kinds of work. Rather than following a single fixed route, she used new training and research opportunities to develop her interests. This brief profile retains only the clearly described elements of the transcript.
