Elyse Watkins

A summer that opened up a scientific community

Elyse Watkins’s international research experience began with a ThinkSwiss-supported summer in Sai Reddy’s laboratory at ETH Zurich in Basel. It became an important example of a welcoming, interdisciplinary scientific community.

The work

By the time of the interview, she was a molecular engineering doctoral student at the University of Chicago, researching protein therapeutics in a systems and synthetic immunology laboratory. Her earlier training included biomedical engineering at Georgia Tech.

The perspective

She valued collaboration across disciplines and a laboratory culture in which people supported one another and shared credit. This is an international biomedical engineering story from the broader EverydayBME archive; her Swiss exchange was supported by ThinkSwiss, rather than Whitaker.
