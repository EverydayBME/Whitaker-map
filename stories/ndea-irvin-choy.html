N’Dea Irvin-Choy

Connecting scientific ambition and public conversation

N’Dea Irvin-Choy’s early interest in chemistry and mathematics included the idea of creating a cosmetics line. Biomedical engineering at Rowan offered a route that brought those scientific interests together.

The work

At Delaware, her doctoral research investigated nanoparticles in the context of pregnancy complications. The project explored targeted delivery as a research question, rather than describing an established treatment.

The perspective

Her interview also connected scientific training with communication beyond the laboratory. Sharing experiences as a biomedical engineer was part of how she helped others understand the field and imagine a place within it. Her path demonstrates how an initial interest can evolve while retaining its underlying curiosity.
