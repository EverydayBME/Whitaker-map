Stacy Skaalure

Materials that respond to their surroundings

Stacy Skaalure’s research at Imperial developed from training in chemical and biological engineering. A Whitaker experience was followed by further international research support, allowing her work in the Stevens group to continue.

The work

Her interests included hydrogels for tissue engineering, three-dimensional cell cultures, and materials designed to respond to cues such as light or enzymes. These approaches connected the properties of an engineered material with the environment in which it would function.

The perspective

Skaalure described a collaborative setting where learning new methods helped broaden an existing research foundation. Her story shows how an international appointment can become more than a short visit, creating space for a scientific direction to develop over several years.
