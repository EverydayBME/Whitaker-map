The experience

Alexis Krueger’s experience in Maura Casadio’s laboratory at Genoa included mentoring undergraduate students. That responsibility helped reveal an interest in teaching alongside her engineering work.

The perspective

Writing in 2020 from a systems-test-engineering role at Exact Sciences, she reflected on a definition of success that included fulfillment and family. Her story leaves room for career directions to change as experience clarifies what matters.
