Geoffrey Handsfield

Making models useful—and research reproducible

Geoffrey Handsfield’s Whitaker postdoctoral experience at Auckland developed into a research-fellow role. His work used computational approaches to understand muscle and other complex biological systems.

The work

In his interview, he discussed the promise of human-organ models for clinical research and drug development. He also emphasized a challenge shared by experiments and computation: complex methods can be difficult for other researchers to reproduce.

The perspective

His 2020 alumni reflection connected international experience with personal growth and a broader view of science. He encouraged high standards without perfectionism: research needs to become available for others to use. The value of a model lies partly in whether it can support work beyond the group that created it.
