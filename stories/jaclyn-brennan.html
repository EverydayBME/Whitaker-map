Jaclyn Brennan

A year to ask new questions

After a master’s experience in tissue engineering, Jaclyn Brennan still had research questions she wanted to explore. A Whitaker fellowship at École Polytechnique offered time to pursue them while living in France.

The work

The fellowship gave her an opportunity to develop greater independence in research without immediately committing to a doctoral program. Working in a laboratory with mathematicians, physicists, and biologists also showed her how an engineering perspective could contribute to a broader team.

The perspective

Her decision combined scientific curiosity with a wish to experience another culture. In the interview, the value of the year lay in both: space to test a research direction and the daily experience of learning with people whose training differed from her own.

“I was able to bring my own cultural perspectives to the group, and I also felt valued as a scientist because I was an engineer.”

— Jaclyn Brennan, archived interview excerpt
