Background and career path

The hardest part of my career has been overcoming cultural boundaries abroad. I currently work in Europe, which has a very different work culture to the USA. Each state within the EU has its own micro work culture.

The international experience

International education was important to me because I wanted to grow in this type of setting. I never felt I wanted to work in a 'one country' work environment. I chose my host institution because a great mentor I had in the USA (Arash Kheradvar) had a collaboration with a researcher in Italy. The Whitaker fellowship changed my future entirely. I always believed I would be working in USA, or my home country, Puerto Rico, but with the fellowship I traveled abroad to Italy and never returned (I've been 10 years in Italy).

Work and career reflections

I work on cardiovascular medical devices, managing European research projects for the R&D USA team. People would be surprised to know my specific field of work is completely different from my educational background and initial career goals. I would tell a younger version of myself to just go for it, don't hold back on any opportunity thrown your way.
