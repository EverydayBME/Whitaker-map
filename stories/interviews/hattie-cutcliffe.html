Background and career path

My current advisor, Dr. Lou DeFrate, influenced me the most in science. He really impressed upon me that, "It's not called 'search'--it's called 'research'--for a reason!" This helped me appreciate that our current scientific understanding is constantly evolving, and helped me see unexpected results as exciting challenges rather than demoralizing obstacles. Furthermore, his humorous, patient, and inquisitive approach to all problems (scientific or not) was inspiring, and was a daily reminder for me to not take life too seriously.

The international experience

I will never forget my host advisor, Dr. Karin Brolin. She is such a capable, logical, and caring person--not to mention an outstanding scientist! She is definitely a huge role model for me both professionally and personally. I very much appreciated my time working for her, learning from her, and spending time getting to know her and her family. Tak, Karin!

Work and career reflections

The most important piece of career advice I would share with a younger version of myself is to not be afraid to speak up if you feel something is wrong or if a current situation is not working for you. You are your biggest advocate and ally, and things won't get better until you take the first step by speaking up about your feelings. (Also, no matter how alone you may feel or unique you perceive your situation to be, you'd be surprised by the number of people who have gone through very similar experiences!)
