What is something you miss about the US that you don't get in Genoa ... or Italy?

I think that in the US, or at least at the Marquette campus, there are more activity group -activity that the university organize- and feel more the university atmosphere-

Would you say spirit?

Yeah. Something like that. Here, there is no strong feeling of being part of something bigger. On a US campus, just walking around there you feel part of the university and everyone seems very proud to be there. Here you don’t feel in the same way.

What is something that you're struggling with right now? What's like your biggest struggle?

Oh, right now I’m struggling to define my project. Here, during your first year as PhD student you have to define your research project. Right now I have decided the field in which I would work on, which is the sensory substitution, but there are still too many options and I don't know how to manage it.

Where do you hope to go with your career?

Let’s just take this one step at a time.
