Gastone Ciuti

Scuola Superiore Sant'Anna - BioRobotics Institute

5 April 2019 · 1 minute read

“Our job is great even though we work a lot! We do not have strict and imposed time schedules; but sometimes it's difficult to separate private and working life. But because we really like the work we do, I think that we are super lucky to do this job. You have the chance to contribute new scientific principles, new technologies, and new innovation that can improve the overall quality of life for people around the world.”

“I would say that I found my way day-by-day, I was not a genius. I'm not a genius. I'm a person that really likes what he does, and I truly believe that what I’m doing can contribute to our society. This motivates me to work hard every day. I see myself as a small part of a larger innovation “machine”; my contributions might be small but hopefully fundamental towards a positive future.

“Pisa is a magic city for research and for innovation. Pisa is a kind of “Cambridge”, in the sense it's a melting pot of universities with incredibly talented students and researchers. When you go around Pisa you may find the next Nobel prize winner. I suggest that students come here to study, to grow, and to start their careers—whether in the academic or in the industrial fields.”
