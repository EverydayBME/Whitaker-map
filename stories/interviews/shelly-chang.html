Shelly Chang

Academia Sinica - Institute of Biomedical Sciences

January 2019 · 1 minute read

The longer interview text from the edited EverydayBME source document. Administrative headers and duplicate social-post extracts have been omitted; the interview wording is retained. Statements and roles reflect the original interview, not current appointments or research outcomes.

How did you get interested in BME? And Can you tell us about what made you decide to pursue a PhD?

“I think I need to trace it back to my high school, one of my biological teachers. She went to US to get her Master's degree. So she taught us in a different way, especially in doing research. She told us a lot of funny experiments. So that's why she inspired my interest in biology. So I choose biology as my major in college…”

This led her to where she is now, doing drug-development for the human body.

“Right now we have significant results of my project, especially because the drug is very related to the human, so this drug's already in clinical use. It's not just a clinical trial. It's already used in some patients. So because now we know antibody effect therapeutics, so they've made me feel very excited that I really did something good for patients, and I am excited to continue studying the effects of these antibodies.”

Would you be able to tell us more about a time where a mentor helped change or influence the trajectory of your scientific career?

I think that, my advisor right now really impressed me a lot because before I got my master degree, I didn't really learn too much about how to do research. But when I came here as a PhD student here, I really started knowing how to do research. I think in logical thinking and ways, the position I am in right now has really first influenced me a lot. Especially the question I'm working on right now is actually, I prefer to do the applied science now, the basic science. So this lab studying at really fits my career goal in the future. So I can say that my PI as a mentor really influenced me a lot, especially, since I've been here, my English speaking improved.
