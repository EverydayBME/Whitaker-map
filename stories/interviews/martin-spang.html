Background and career path

It's not just one moment, but I truly enjoy spending time with people in my different labs. They are some of my closest friends and help me get through some tough times. Grabbing a drink or having a barbecue after a tough week in lab with some of your closest colleagues is priceless.

The international experience

a) It is so important to get outside your comfort zone. You experience the most growth when you are uncomfortable. Moving to a country where you don't know a single person was incredibly tough at the time, but completely worth it for my personal growth and the friends I made along the way. e) I don't think I would have ever left my home state of Ohio if it wasn't for the Whitaker program. I'm currently in California finishing up my PhD, and I don't know where I will be next year.

Work and career reflections

a) I am currently finishing up my PhD in bioengineering at UC San Diego, developing injectable hydrogels to repair the heart after a heart attack. d) Keep an open mind and be willing to try anything. Get to know people and get outside your comfort zone as much as possible.
