Background and career path

The best thing about being a BME for me is learning how our research and technologies impact the world. BMEs really can make a difference across many cultures and continents!

The international experience

During my evenings, I had the opportunity to volunteer as a tutor in low-income communities in Cape Town with the Amy Foundation. I will never forget the amazing and strong children I worked with there! I always wanted to live abroad and Whitaker gave me that opportunity while still working towards my BME career goals. After that experience I realized I wanted more and I lived in Tanzania for the following year on a Fulbright US Scholar program. I wouldn't have had the guts to do that without having my Whitaker experience first.

Work and career reflections

Right now I am an Assistant Professor in BME at the University of Delaware. I love working with my students and showing them how BME can impact people around the world! Go for it! Even when it's hard, take the initiative to push yourself. Now is the time to explore and have new experiences.
