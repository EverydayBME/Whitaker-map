Lauren Ayton

Research shaped around people with vision loss

Lauren Ayton’s work brought clinical experience and research together around severe vision impairment. At the time of her interview, she held roles spanning the University of Melbourne and the Royal Victorian Eye and Ear Hospital.

The work

Her research interests included bionic eyes, gene therapy, assistive technology, and understanding the course of eye disease. Clinical measurements and natural-history studies helped create the foundation needed to design and evaluate research involving patients.

The perspective

The interview highlights the breadth of a translational research team. Developing a technology is only part of the work; understanding people’s needs, collecting meaningful information, and supporting clinical studies are essential parts of investigating whether an idea may be useful.
