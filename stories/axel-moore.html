Axel Moore

Expanding the toolkit for cartilage research

Axel Moore’s background in mechanical and biomedical engineering led toward questions about cartilage, including how it bears load and provides lubrication. His international research experience helped broaden the methods he could bring to those problems.

Institution / community

The work

At Imperial’s Stevens group, his work connected mechanical questions with biomaterials and cell culture. Learning those approaches meant working across disciplines rather than treating cartilage as only a mechanical system.

The perspective

Moore’s interview described a path shaped by discovering opportunities, pursuing further training, and mentoring students. A new laboratory could provide both a research project and a chance to build capabilities that would change the questions he was able to ask.
