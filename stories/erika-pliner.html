The experience

Erika Pliner’s Whitaker experience at NeuRA developed into further research there. Her work connected human movement and earlier occupational research with questions about ladder use and falls among older adults.

The perspective

In her 2020 reflection, she described the interaction of physical, cognitive, and psychological factors. The international experience offered a setting in which biomechanics could meet a wider view of human behavior and the practical situations in which injuries occur.
