Angus Johnston

Following a material into the cell

Angus Johnston’s research brought together physical chemistry, molecular biology, and nanoengineered materials. At Monash, he investigated what happens when engineered materials interact with cells.

The work

That work included questions about cellular trafficking and targeted delivery, alongside methods for controlling how proteins are presented on a material. Understanding those interactions required moving between the properties of a designed system and the behavior of a living cell.

The perspective

His teaching and research crossed disciplinary boundaries. The story offers an example of how engineering at very small scales still depends on a broad scientific toolkit—and on asking what a material actually does in its biological setting.
