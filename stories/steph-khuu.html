Steph Khuu

Connecting muscle experiments and computer models

Steph Khuu’s research at the Auckland Bioengineering Institute investigated muscle regeneration in the context of cerebral palsy. Her project combined computational modeling with experimental work.

The work

Her earlier physiology training included neurophysiology and laboratory work on brain cells. She also spent time on the BabyX digital-brain project before moving into muscle research. These experiences connected biological questions with tools from engineering and computation.

The perspective

The interview shows a career developing through connections between fields. Building models and testing them against experiments offered complementary ways to understand a complex biological system, with each approach helping to sharpen questions for the other.

“Biomedical engineers are the kinds of people who are cross-disciplinary in putting everything together to solve problems that humans have.”

— Steph Khuu, archived interview excerpt
