Marloes Groot

Taking an imaging idea toward the hospital

Marloes Groot’s research used light to investigate biological tissue. Her path from photosynthesis and laser research toward medical imaging developed through collaborations with people asking clinical questions.

The work

At VU Amsterdam, her group worked with higher-harmonic microscopy, including efforts to move from a laboratory system toward equipment that could be used in a hospital. Brain and lung collaborations helped connect technical development with different tissue-imaging needs.

The perspective

Groot emphasized the value of good supervision and projects that are feasible to carry out. Her account shows how a technology gains direction through the people who may use it, as well as through advances in the instrument itself.
