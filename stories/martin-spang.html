The experience

Martin Spang’s Whitaker experience at Cardiff helped him grow more confident in an unfamiliar environment. Friendships formed around the laboratory became a lasting part of that experience.

The perspective

By his 2020 reflection, he was a doctoral researcher at UC San Diego investigating injectable hydrogels for heart-repair research. His account connected scientific development with the confidence and relationships that come from learning to work abroad.
