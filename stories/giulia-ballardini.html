Giulia Ballardini

From biomaterials to sensory feedback

Giulia Ballardini’s studies took her from biomedical engineering in Bologna to neuroengineering in Genoa. An experience at Marquette University helped her see how those interests could become hands-on rehabilitation research.

The work

Working with vibrotactile feedback for people recovering from stroke connected her engineering training with rehabilitation research. By the time of her interview, she had chosen sensory substitution as a field for her doctorate and was working to define a specific project within it.

The perspective

Her interview paired enthusiasm for meaningful research with an appreciation of campus community. Rather than describing a career planned from the beginning, she described learning through each stage and taking the next decision one step at a time.

“Let’s just take this one step at a time.”

— Giulia Ballardini, archived interview excerpt
