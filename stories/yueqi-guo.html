Yueqi Guo

Seeing neural activity through an engineering lens

Yueqi Guo’s interest in both medicine and engineering led from undergraduate study at Tsinghua University to graduate study at Johns Hopkins. Her research connected optical imaging with questions about the brain.

The work

She described studying neural processing of sound in marmosets. The project brought together imaging methods and neuroscience, using engineering tools to investigate how a biological system handles information.

The perspective

Her account also emphasized the community around the research. Help from others made adapting to a new environment easier, and lively laboratory discussions made scientific work intellectually engaging. The experience of learning abroad was social as well as technical.
