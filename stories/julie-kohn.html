The experience

Julie Kohn’s Whitaker experience in Cape Town included research with Thomas Franz and volunteer tutoring through the Amy Foundation. Both experiences helped shape her interest in education and global biomedical engineering.

The perspective

A later Fulbright experience in Tanzania continued that direction. Writing in 2020 from an assistant-professor role at the University of Delaware, she reflected on a path in which teaching and international engagement had become closely connected.
