When an exchange changes the long-term plan

Jan Mangual received a Whitaker grant in 2011 and shared this reflection with EverydayBME in 2020. The experience was hosted by University of Florence.

The experience

Jan Mangual’s Whitaker postdoctoral experience in Florence became a turning point in his international career. He reflected on the influence of mentors and the relationships built through research in Italy.

The perspective

Writing in 2020 from a scientist role at Abbott, he connected that experience with subsequent work in European cardiovascular-device research and development. His story illustrates how an exchange intended as one stage of training can shape much longer-term professional choices.
