Hannah Meredith

Bringing engineering questions into global health

Hannah Meredith’s path from biomedical engineering to public health grew through fieldwork and international collaboration. After a Duke doctorate, she pursued a Whitaker experience at the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine.

The work

An earlier field experience in Sri Lanka helped connect her quantitative training with public health questions. By the time of her interview, she was a postdoctoral researcher at Johns Hopkins studying human mobility and malaria.

The perspective

Meredith chose her London host with attention to collaborators, resources, and opportunities for fieldwork. Her story shows how engineering training can travel beyond a traditional engineering department, providing tools for questions that depend on understanding people and places as well as data.
