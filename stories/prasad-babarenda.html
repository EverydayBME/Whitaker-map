Prasad Babarenda

Modeling how soft tissues change shape

Prasad Babarenda’s postdoctoral work at the Auckland Bioengineering Institute focused on the large deformations of soft tissues. Breast biomechanics provided a demanding example of why those changes matter.

The work

When a patient’s position changes between imaging or clinical procedures, the breast changes shape as well. His modeling work addressed how to relate tissue locations across those different configurations, connecting mechanics with a practical imaging challenge.

The perspective

The interview also looked beyond a single project toward academic life. Mentoring and developing research proposals were part of the path he was considering, alongside the technical work of building and understanding computational models.
