Gabriella Nilssonhall

Finding a research direction through exchange

Gabriella Nilssonhall’s training took her from Sweden to an Erasmus experience in France and then thesis research in Belgium. That sequence led toward doctoral work in tissue engineering at KU Leuven.

The work

She described the practical work of cell culture and staining alongside the experience of helping other researchers learn. An interest sparked by school science and later coursework developed into the more open-ended questions of a research project.

The perspective

Her story highlights the way an exchange or thesis placement can help reveal a possible next step. Trying a new research environment provided information about both the science and the daily experience of doing it.
