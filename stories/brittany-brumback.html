Brittany Brumback

Making an international semester possible

Brittany Brumback used a Whitaker undergraduate grant to study for a semester at the University of Melbourne. By the time of her interview, she was a doctoral candidate at Washington University in St. Louis.

The work

The grant supported an educational experience tied to her biomedical engineering degree. Arranging it required navigating course approvals and learning how a different university organized its teaching. Some of those practical details became clearer only through conversations with people who knew the system.

The perspective

She encouraged students to seek advice from study-abroad staff, previous participants, and people at the host institution. Her experience also helped make the route easier for later students. A worthwhile opportunity might require persistence and preparation before it becomes an established path.
