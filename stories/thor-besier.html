Thor Besier

Understanding movement through models and collaboration

Thor Besier’s work at Auckland connected musculoskeletal biomechanics with computational modeling. In his interview, he reflected on an international research community that included several Whitaker visitors.

The work

His research explored human movement and the mechanical demands placed on the body. Models offered a way to connect measurements with questions about the structures and forces involved in movement.

The perspective

Besier remembered the Whitaker program as a valuable route for researchers to join projects abroad. His conversation ranged across disciplines and applications, reflecting an environment where new questions often emerged through exchanges between people with different technical backgrounds.
