Fleur O’Hare

The people and planning behind clinical research

Fleur O’Hare described clinical research coordination as the framework that allows a study to happen. Her role connected research plans with the participants whose time and experiences make those studies possible.

Read the edited interview →

Archive date
2019
Institution / community
Melbourne vision research community
Role at the time
Senior clinical research coordinator

The work

That framework included ethics, protocols, enrollment, and attention to participant safety and wellbeing. With a clinical background, she also contributed to assessments, analysis, and communicating results.

The perspective

Her interests extended into the relationship between vision, movement, and the arts, including work with dancers. The interview reveals how a person’s interests beyond the laboratory can help shape the questions they ask within it.
