Frank Luyten

Keeping basic science close to patient needs

Frank Luyten’s career combined clinical rheumatology with a foundation in biochemistry and research. After training in Ghent, he spent eleven years at the U.S. National Institutes of Health before returning to Belgium.

The work

At KU Leuven, his interests included regenerative medicine and cartilage repair. The work connected basic biological questions with the challenges encountered in caring for people with joint disease.

The perspective

His interview presented international experience as part of a long research trajectory. Moving between institutions and between clinical and laboratory work offered different ways to understand a problem, while patient needs remained a reason to pursue the underlying science.
