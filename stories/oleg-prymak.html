Oleg Prymak

Using materials science to ask biological questions

Oleg Prymak’s international path began in solid-state physics in Ukraine and continued through doctoral research in Germany. Biomaterials became a connection between that physical-science background and biomedical engineering.

Institution / community

The work

At Duisburg-Essen, he led work in X-ray diffraction and participated in projects involving materials such as bone-related ceramics and nanoparticles. Understanding material structure provided a foundation for investigating their properties and possible uses.

The perspective

His story reflects the importance of methods that serve many kinds of research. Expertise in characterizing a material can become a bridge between laboratories, countries, and applications, allowing a physicist’s questions to contribute to biological and medical projects.
