The experience

Sydney Geissler’s Whitaker experience brought her to Andreas Hierlemann’s group at ETH Zurich in Basel. The exchange offered a broader perspective on scientific work and the people who make it possible.

The perspective

In her 2020 reflection, she emphasized mentoring as a reciprocal relationship in which both people learn. Writing from a medical-writing role at Medtronic, she also allowed space for uncertainty: a career can develop without every later step being known at the beginning.
