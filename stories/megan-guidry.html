Megan Guidry

From heart research to helping researchers learn

Megan Guidry’s Whitaker award brought her to New Zealand in early 2015. Her initial research examined calcium and the mechanics of the heart’s left ventricle; her later role supported researchers across many fields.

The work

At the time of the interview, she coordinated training for New Zealand eScience Infrastructure and worked with the Carpentries community. The focus included coding and data-management skills that help researchers do their work more effectively.

The perspective

Her story expands the picture of what a research career can become. Experience in a scientific project can also provide a foundation for teaching, building communities, and helping other researchers develop the practical skills they need.
