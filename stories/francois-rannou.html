François Rannou

Connecting joint mechanics with clinical questions

François Rannou’s career linked clinical care with molecular and cellular research on joint disease. At the time of his interview, he worked within the Paris Descartes and Cochin Hospital community.

The work

After medical and doctoral training, research experiences at UC Riverside and UCLA introduced approaches that he could connect with cartilage biology. Mechanotransduction—the way cells respond to physical signals—offered a bridge between mechanical questions and disease research.

The perspective

His account illustrates how methods can travel between scientific areas and across countries. Combining clinical experience with basic science helped shape questions about osteoarthritis and other joint conditions, while keeping the research connected to problems encountered in patient care.
