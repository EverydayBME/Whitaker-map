Itamar Ronen

From chemistry to questions about living tissue

Itamar Ronen’s path into medical imaging began in chemistry and developed through research in Minnesota, Boston, and the Netherlands. At Leiden University Medical Center, he worked on MRI methods for understanding tissue.

Read the edited interview →

Archive date
7 May 2019
Institution / community
Leiden University Medical Center
Role at the time
Associate professor of radiology; MRI researcher

The work

His interests included spectroscopy and diffusion measurements that investigate tissue microstructure. The work connected physical and chemical principles with questions about biological organization and disease.

The perspective

Ronen’s interview reflected the influence of working in different research and clinical environments. Encounters with patient-oriented work helped shape the questions he wanted to pursue, while a foundation in the physical sciences supplied tools for investigating them.
